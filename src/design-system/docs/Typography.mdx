# Typography

**Archivo** carries everything you read. Its width axis is part of the hierarchy: body copy at 100%, sub-heads and buttons at 112%, display and marginal notes at 125%. **Bodoni Moda** — the serif of the monogram’s L — is reserved for figures and quotations.
