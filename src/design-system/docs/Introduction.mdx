# Leeladhar design system

The visual language of Leeladhar Law Chambers, built from seven references:

- **Mosey** (compliance platform) — the cream background `#F3F1ED`, floating matter notes, the role-based tabs.
- **Legal Point** (landing page, branding, conference asset) — the wide grotesk voice, the orbital lines and nodes, the stat rings, the dark numbered service list, the FAQ cards, and the mint `#62E8B3` sampled from the conference stage-floor glow.
- **Creston, BYLAW, Lexiq** — the team section structure and the restraint of a serif used for evidence.

## Principles

1. **Cream is the room, mint is the light.** Mint appears only where something is live or can be acted on.
2. **Archivo speaks, Bodoni testifies.** Archivo for everything you read, stretched wider as it grows in importance. Bodoni Moda — the serif of the monogram’s L — only for figures and quotations.
3. **Lines connect.** Orbital lines link related facts. If a line joins nothing, remove it.
4. **Radius follows size.** Pills for things you press, 14px photographs, 20px panels, 32px dark blocks.
